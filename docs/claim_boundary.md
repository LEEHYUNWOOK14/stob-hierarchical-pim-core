# Claim boundary

- Functional claims are limited to the included RTL benches and their recorded inputs.
- Physical claims are limited to the stated OpenROAD/Sky130HD research evidence.
- Vectorless power is not workload activity power or energy.
- A GR00T-derived profile capture is not full GR00T end-to-end inference.
- The repository does not redistribute the Samsung PIMSimulator/DRAMSim2-based C++ simulator.
