# Contribution and review rules

- Do not add `PIMSimulator/src`, `libdramsim`, `sim`, `obj_dir`, or thermal code.
- Preserve original copyright and third-party attribution.
- Keep measured, simulated, modeled, estimated, and illustrative evidence classes distinct.
- Do not change a historical failure into PASS; add a dated superseding result.
- Do not claim timing closure, signoff, manufacturing readiness, or full-model acceleration from this package.
